We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, applicable legal obligations, and legitimate recordkeeping. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle, although no electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or object to certain uses of your information, subject to applicable law. You can also opt out of promotional email communications at any time.

We may update this policy as our services or practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
